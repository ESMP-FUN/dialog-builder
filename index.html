Dialog Builder

for Minecraft Java 1.21.6+ & Paper

Drag a field from the right into the dialog, or click it to drop one on the end. Click anything here to edit it — the toggles, sliders and choice buttons work, and what you set is the value the player starts with.

Why can't I put two things side by side? Because the game will not. A dialog is one centred column: everything to read, then everything to fill in, then the buttons. The only layout choices you have are the order and how many buttons fit on a row.
